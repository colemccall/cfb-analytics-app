Team Performance Evaluator

Residual = actual SP+ minus expected SP+ predicted from 3-year recruiting composite. Positive = overachiever, negative = underachiever.

Recruiting Class ROI

Hit%/Rated = share of rated recruits who reached peak OVR ≥ 75. Hit%/Class = same metric vs all recruits (exposes unrated player coverage gaps).

Breakout Candidates (Engine D)

XGBoost regressor trained on 2008–2022 player seasons predicts next-season OVR. Breakout = predicted ≥ +5 OVR. Decline = predicted ≤ −5 OVR. Test MAE ≈ 9 OVR points.
